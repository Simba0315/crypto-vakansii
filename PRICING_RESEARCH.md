# Pricing research: how job sites and job-search services show their prices

Research date: 13 November 2026. Every page named below was opened on that date, unless the line says "not opened" or "secondary".

This file adds to `COMPANIES_RESEARCH.md` and `SOURCES_RESEARCH.md`. It does not repeat their legal-entity tables. A figure from those files is used here only where I opened the page again today.

How to read the marks:

- **(browser)** = I saw the page rendered in a browser window about 800 pixels wide.
- **(text)** = I read the page through a text reader. It gives words and order, not colours or exact placement.
- **secondary** = the figure is not from the site itself. The source is named. Treat it as unverified.
- **not opened** = the site showed a bot check, a login wall, a region block or an error. I did not try to get around it.
- **my arithmetic** = I calculated it. The inputs are shown.

Location: the requests left from Vietnam. Two sites showed prices in Vietnamese dong because of that. One site (Enhancv) showed dollar prices lower than its own page title. All other prices were shown in US dollars or rubles as written here. I saw them from one location only, so I cannot say that they are the same everywhere.

Currency conversion, used only where stated: 1 US dollar = 25,690.62 dong = 82.85 rubles (open.er-api.com, rate of 13 Nov 2026).

I created no accounts, started no checkouts and entered no data.

---

## Summary (10 lines)

1. Job-seeker services almost all sell an auto-renewing subscription. The median price of one month, bought month by month, is 29 US dollars (11 sites, range 7.64 to 49.95).
2. Ten of 14 seeker pricing pages show exactly three paid choices. Seven use a "Most popular" badge, and it usually sits on the middle choice (1 month or 3 months), not on the longest plan.
3. None of the 14 uses a classic monthly/annual switch. Nine show each period as its own card. Three use a three-way period switch (Huntr, Enhancv, hh PRO).
4. Longer plans are shown as a small "per month" (or per day, per week) figure with the real charge in smaller text. Quarterly plans work out at a median of about 21.5 dollars a month (8 sites).
5. A cheap paid trial that renews at 8 to 14 times the price is used by FlexJobs, Resume.io, Zety and JobAssist. Three of them bill "every 4 weeks", which is 13 charges a year.
6. Niche job boards publish one fixed price for a job post. The median for a single 30-day post is 299 dollars (11 sites, range 150 to 399). Six of the 11 charge exactly 299.
7. Large general sites publish no price at all: Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Jooble and Welcome to the Jungle show a budget model or "contact sales".
8. Job boards use two layouts: plan cards (We Work Remotely, Working Nomads, Jobicy, Dice, Wellfound) or one posting form with paid add-ons and a live total on the button (Remote OK, Web3.career, Remote3, CryptoJobsList).
9. On the form layout, the total on the button starts above the advertised price because add-ons are ticked in advance: 447 against "from 299" (Remote OK), 695 against an implied 299 (Web3.career), 349 against 199 (Remote3), 1,683 against a 200 budget (CryptoJobsList).
10. Crypto payment is stated on only two pricing pages: Cryptocurrency Jobs ("Crypto payment accepted") and CryptoJobsList (BTC, ETH, USDC, Doge, "select customers only").

### Layout of seeker pricing pages

Counted on the 14 seeker pricing pages I opened: JobAssist, FlexJobs, RemoteJobsFinder, LazyApply, Teal, Jobscan, Huntr, Rezi, Kickresume, Resume.io, Zety, Enhancv, Hirify, hh PRO.

- **Three paid choices is the norm: 10 of 14.** JobAssist, FlexJobs, RemoteJobsFinder, LazyApply, Teal, Huntr, Kickresume, Enhancv, Hirify, hh PRO. The other four show two paid choices for one person (Jobscan, Resume.io, Zety, Rezi).
- **A free level sits beside the paid ones on 8 of 14.** Teal, Jobscan, Huntr, Rezi, Kickresume, Resume.io, Zety, Enhancv.
- **A "most popular" badge is on 7 of 14.** JobAssist, FlexJobs, RemoteJobsFinder, LazyApply, Kickresume, Zety, hh PRO. On the first five it marks the middle choice, not the longest plan. Zety and LazyApply add a second badge ("BEST VALUE", "10X Value").

- **The main button names the product, not the payment.** "Build My Resume", "Build Resume", "Start finding jobs", "Become a FlexJobs Member", "Get started", "Select Plan", "Choose plan", "Try 7 Days Free", "Выбрать", "Подключить". Only Rezi's lifetime offer says "Buy for $149".
