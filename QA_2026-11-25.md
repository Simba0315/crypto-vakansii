## Ghost listings: is an open job still open on the employer's own page? (26 Nov, 02:30 to 04:00 UTC)

The promise: only jobs still open on the company's careers page; a listing disappears once the company takes it down. Measured on the
live site, read-only: our own pages and sitemaps, then the feeds collect.py reads, at most two requests a second per host, 10 s timeouts,
our own User-Agent, nothing behind a bot check (a 403 or a challenge would count as unknown; there were none).

- **Sample**: 430 open jobs, job-weighted at random from the 44 job sitemaps (864,491 addresses; 59 of the 489 pages read were already
  marked closed), within 18 hiring systems: Workday 45, Greenhouse 45, Ashby 40, Paylocity, iCIMS, JazzHR, BambooHR and Workable 30 each,
  Lever 25, Breezy and Rippling 20, USAJOBS 15, Pinpoint, Gem, Recruitee, Personio and Teamtailor 12, Trakstar 10. Checked through the
  Greenhouse and Lever job APIs, the Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, Teamtailor, Personio, BambooHR, Breezy, JazzHR, Paylocity, iCIMS, Rippling,
  Pinpoint, Gem and Trakstar lists, Workday's posting JSON (`posted`, `canApply`) and USAJOBS' historic API. Six first-pass unknowns or
  checker mistakes (Recruitee slugs, a myworkdaysite.com address, a Lever timeout, a Trakstar address) were checked by hand: all open.
- **Stale rate: 1 of 430 (0.23 %; 95 % upper bound about 1.3 %).** 0 in every system but BambooHR (1 of 30): Discovery Kidzone's "Part Time
  Teacher – Tues/Thurs" was gone from its list (its detail answers 404), our page said open, last checked 24 Nov; the board had dropped about
  8 of our 25 jobs and added new ones the site did not have.
- **Whole boards**: 184 boards of the sample, our open jobs against the board's list now: ours exceed theirs on 4 boards, by 11 of 12,157
  (0.09 %, a lower bound: titles are cleaned and one row stands for a role in several places, so only counts were compared).
- **Telegram and employer-posted jobs**: none on the site (the channel cryptojobslist was switched on today; `/api/public/export.json` lists
  0 posted jobs). Telegram posts are never read again: open TG_DAYS = 30 days after the post.
- **Boards that no longer exist** (rare, so not in a random sample; found on their own): a board whose hiring system answers "no such board"
  came back as "did not answer", and that keeps every job open for good, Apply leading to a 404. Of the 2,589 companies whose newest sitemap
  date was 23 Nov or older: Ashby 11 of 18 gone, Greenhouse 10 of 16, BambooHR 1 of 60 asked (of 530; suspended or expired accounts), none
  of 60 asked on Paylocity, JazzHR, Workday, iCIMS, Rippling and Breezy. About 400 to 500 listings: Klaviyo 149 (Ashby 404 since 16 Nov),
  Norm Ai 28, Headout 19 (since 17 Nov), SHEIN 16, Kunai 10, AI Squared 8, Oso 7; all 15 boards in /status/'s "did not answer" list were gone.
- **"Last checked" on the pages**: today 30 %, yesterday 41 %, 2 days 22 %, 3 to 8 days 7 % (Workday boards of Vermeer, Intapp and TJX: 18 Nov).
  It was the day of the last full collecting run that read the board. Those ran 2 to 5 times a day (25 Nov: 01:40, 06:06, 11:11, 15:09,
  19:23 UTC; 24 Nov only 07:18 and 21:01): of the last 300 workflow runs 223 were cancelled (a newer push replaces the waiting run) and
  only 4 scheduled runs finished; pushes collect only when the saved jobs are over three hours old. A rotating board is read in one run of
  4 (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever lists) or 8 (Workday, BambooHR, Paylocity, JazzHR, Breezy, Rippling, iCIMS).
- **New jobs**: on 85 Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever boards of the sample, 61 of the 175 postings published 2 to 24 hours earlier (35 %) were not
  on the site yet; 12 of 835 published 1 to 7 days earlier (1.4 %).
- **The live reader was down**: `hrn-live.service` (live.py, the program meant to read every board about hourly between uploads) had been in
  a crash loop since the upload of 25 Nov 21:02 UTC, 176 restarts by 03:46 UTC, each ending in `reload()` with `KeyError: 'title'`: a live
  row that only notes a closing ("closed_only", no title) for a job the new hourly record no longer holds at all. Nothing was found or closed
  between uploads for about seven hours; the newest job `/api/latest` showed as found live was from 21:02:32 UTC, and live-found jobs fell
  out of search at the next index load because the reader never put them back. That is why new jobs took up to a day and the closings above.

Fixed (tests: `python3 closing_test.py`, `python3 live.py --test`, `python3 telegram_jobs.py --test`; collect.RULES unchanged, 21447375):

- live.py `reload()`: such rows are dropped (there is no page left to mark closed) and a live row without a title or with bad JSON can no
  longer stop the program. Live after the next upload; the reader then restarts with the new code.
- collect.py `close_gone()` + `board_gone()` (+ `net.probe`): a board that failed this run, none of whose open jobs was seen on a read for
  two days (GONE_DAYS), is asked once whether it exists. Only a definite "no such board" counts: 404 from its list (Workday: 404 with its
  error code, iCIMS: its "gone:" page), BambooHR or JazzHR sending the address to their home page or to a suspended/expired account, Paylocity
  to JobNotFound, Trakstar "Invalid client name". Then its jobs close and `/status/` lists it under "Job boards that no longer exist" with the
  number closed. Timeouts, 403, 500 and odd pages change nothing, nor does one system saying it of many boards at once (DOWN_MIN, DOWN_SHARE).
  Checked against the live systems: the 15 failing boards of the last run all answer "gone"; 1inch (Lever) and Anthropic (Greenhouse) do not.
- Job pages' "Last checked" (and "still on the careers page when we last checked, on ...") now take the day live.py last read the company's
  board to the end (new table `read_ok` in the live store, `records.live_checked`), when it is later than the hourly record's; an empty
  answer from a board of 20 or more open jobs, which closes nothing, does not count. Tried on a real records file: Nov 13 became Nov 26.
- live.py `sources()`: one board per company name, the one collect.main reads (Aurelian, Fireflies, OneSignal and Imagen Technologies had
  two; a read of one closed the other's jobs and the next read opened them again as new, around the clock).
- Telegram jobs no longer get `last_seen` = today on every run (the posts are never read again).
- Wording: /about/ ("How often": the server's reading around the clock and the rebuilds), the hourly-check guide (intro; step 3 now names
  boards that no longer exist), /status/ (schedule: "about once an hour", what happens when the reader falls behind, a rebuild takes two to
  three hours, what "Last checked" means; failed feeds), /status/, /methodology/ and /safety/ no longer say a job is marked closed "the
  same day" (at the next reading, usually within hours, at most a day or two).

Not changed (rules, a full re-read or a decision):

- 📋 Keep the live reader watched: a crash loop of `hrn-live` went unnoticed for seven hours. Suggested: an alert when its restart counter
  grows (systemd `NRestarts`) or when the newest live `seen_at` is over 30 minutes old, and the same figure on `/status/`.
- 📋 A board that fails for other reasons (timeouts, 403, a page that is no list) still keeps its jobs open for good. A rule such as "not
  read for 7 days: closed, or marked 'not checked since …'" is a policy choice for the owner.
- 📋 Telegram: TG_DAYS = 30 days with no re-check. Reading each post again (`t.me/s/<channel>/<id>` answers without it once deleted) or a
  shorter TG_DAYS would keep channel jobs closer to the truth; the channel was only switched on today.
- 📋 Workday lists stop at 2,000: NVIDIA's board answers `total: 2000` and, past offset 2,000, the same page again and again, so the jobs
  beyond are never read (new ones are not added, ours are closed although open). Hidden, not stale. Reading such a board in parts by a facet
  (location or job family) is a change in fetch_feed, inside collect.RULES: a full re-read.
- 📋 Collecting runs: pushes cancel the waiting scheduled run, and a push collects only when the saved jobs are over three hours old, so the
  full record was collected 2 to 5 times a day. With the live reader healthy that is enough; without it, rotating boards wait one to eight days.
