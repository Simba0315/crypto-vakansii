# QA of the live site, 2026-11-25

Read-only checks of https://jobseveryhour.com from outside (curl, a script, the built-in browser), plus read-only looks at the server's
logs and query plans. Ranked by how much a visitor feels it. ✅ fixed (commit) · 🟡 fixed in code, live after the next upload · 📋 open.

## Found and fixed

1. 🟡 `34d1754` Home page "Latest jobs" and `/new/` never got new rows: `/api/latest` answered 504 after 60 s, every time. Its two queries read the whole records file (3.7 GB, holding the lock job pages also need) and the whole live table (1.9 GB), once a minute per open home page. Indexes `jobs_new` (records.py) and `live_new` (live.py), one answer shared for 15 s (serve.py).
2. 🟡 `219ef7b` Search box gave up on common words: `/api/search?all=1&q=warehouse` (and "software engineer") answered 503 after 3 s, and the page then matched titles only for the rest of the visit. The counts it asked for and never shows took 2.3 to 3.5 s; without them 0.3 to 0.5 s (measured on the server).
3. 🟡 `2141613` `sitemaps/pages.xml` held 80,256 addresses; the limit is 50,000 and Google refuses a bigger file, so the company, place, skill and role pages in it were not offered to search engines. Now split into files of 40,000 (`pages.xml`, `pages-2.xml`, `pages-3.xml`).
4. 🟡 `b7302ac` `/apple-touch-icon.png` (asked for by iPhones and crawlers at the root) was 404. Copied to the root with the build.

## Found, not fixed (needs a decision)

5. 🟡 `76f3747` `/jobs/` downloaded the whole search index before filters worked: 22 MB compressed, 100 MB unpacked, 451 MB of browser memory. Now every search goes to `/api/search?list=1` (`f253ad9`: all the page's filters in Lucene, 50 rows a page, one per role) and the first screen with no filter comes inside the page. `/jobs/?q=nurse&where=Texas` on a cold load: 26.6 MB and about 10 s before, about 0.28 MB and results 0.3 to 0.9 s after the page (measured against a copy of the new index on the server); the page's memory 622 MB before, 3 MB after. `10af7ef`: the home page's For you tab, the fit box on job pages, My jobs, jobs near me and the account ask the server too; For you's full scoring, the resume builder's skill counts and the jobs map still read the big file, on a phone only after a tap.
6. 🟡 Server load: load average 7 to 11 on 2 cores at noon with 24% I/O wait; the page service sits at its 1 GB memory limit (782 MB its own). Items 1 and 2 were a large part of the reads. The nginx log of 25 Nov (00:58 to 12:29 UTC) shows who downloaded the big file: 2,481 times, 2,394 of them Applebot and OAI-SearchBot rendering `/jobs/`, each one 100 MB that nginx compressed on the fly. That is gone with item 5; their searches now cost one Lucene query each (0.1 to 0.5 s), kept a minute in `serve.py` and two in nginx. Check again after the next upload (`uptime`, `systemctl show hrn-site -p MemoryCurrent`).
7. 🟡 `5391706` The footer (on every page, the home page too) linked "Sales & Business Development" to `/jobs/sales-bd/`, a page whose canonical is `/categories/sales/` (one page per query). The footer's four biggest fields now link the page that answers their search, found by the title test one_page_per_query uses; the home page body has no such link.

- For the first 6.5 minutes after the upload (16:31 to 16:38) the search answered 503 "search is being reloaded": `search_engine.ready()` refuses an index loaded by the older code (`_meta.v`), so the first load after a new VERSION leaves /jobs/ without results until the new index is in place; the page says so plainly.
