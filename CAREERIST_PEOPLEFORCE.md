## 1. Careerist (careerist.cc)

**Same company as careerist.com?** Yes. `www.careerist.com` sends a 302 redirect to `https://careerist.cc/`. Source: WebFetch of https://www.careerist.com.

**In one line:** a paid online school that retrains US adults with no tech background for entry-level tech jobs (manual QA, QA automation, AI automation, tech sales). Every course ends with an internship, career coaching and job search help, backed by a money-back job guarantee. Everything free on the site exists to collect a phone number for a sales call.

### 1.2 How it is paid

- **One-time upfront** is pushed hardest: the biggest discount, and the only way to keep the job guarantee. The payments page says about 60% of clients pay upfront. Source: https://careerist.cc/payments.
- **In-house installments**: no interest, up to 4 months, at least 10% first, then 30/60/90 days, with automatic card billing. **Split in 2 or 3** payments within 28 days. Source: same.
- **Loans** through Climb Credit, Affirm and Klarna. The page says 70% of applicants are approved. Loan students lose the guarantee. Source: same.
- **Income share (ISA):** not offered on the payments page. The guarantee text still mentions "ISA students", so it existed before. Source: https://careerist.cc/legal/guarantee/05112026.
- **No subscription** anywhere.
- **Permanent "limited time" discounts.** Every course shows a crossed-out list price and an "Enroll by" date. The upfront price is the real price.

### 1.3 The job guarantee (full rules)

Source: https://careerist.cc/legal/guarantee/05112026 (latest of 11 dated versions since Nov 2021).

- Money back if no qualifying US job within **10 months** after the certificate. The home page still says "within a year".
- The student must send **80 applications a week** in months 1-6 and 40 a week in months 7-10, with screenshots as proof. Attend all 6 coaching calls. Grow LinkedIn to 500+ contacts. Score 90%+ on homework. Be willing to move anywhere in the US. Not change the resume without the coach's approval.
- Out: loan payers, people without US work rights, anyone who turns down a qualifying offer.
- Refund: 100% of tuition paid to Careerist. Fees for financing or card processing are not returned.

The guarantee is mostly a sales tool: the rules are strict enough that few people could claim it.

## 2. PeopleForce (peopleforce.io)

Numbers on the home page (page says): "1,800+ companies", "120,000 active users", "25+ countries", "98%" satisfaction, first reply in "2 min", ISO 27001, data in Frankfurt. Source: https://peopleforce.io. (In April 2025 the news said 1,300+ clients; see `COMPANIES_RESEARCH.md`.)

### 2.2 Pricing model

Source: https://peopleforce.io/pricing.

- **Per employee per month, sold in modules.** Only Core HR has a public price: Standard **$2.5**, Professional **$3** per employee per month.
- **Minimum 50 employees** (so at least $125 a month). Yearly billing is shown as "-20%".
- Recruit, Perform, Pulse, Time, Desk, Payroll, AI: **no public price**, "Add product" goes through sales.
- No free plan. 14-day trial after a demo, no card needed. No onboarding fee. Card or direct debit. Taxes added on top.
- Publishing on job boards where the company has its own contract needs a paid "Contracts add-on". Source: https://peopleforce.io/products/peoplerecruit/job-board-posting.

**How a job board gets in (three doors):**

1. **Build the "job board" integration ourselves.** PeopleForce has a developer guide for exactly this. The employer creates an API key in PeopleForce and gives it to the board. The board reads the employer's open vacancies (`state=opened`), lets the employer pick which to post, and sends each applicant back into PeopleForce with a `source` name. The guide warns the recruitment endpoints are "not yet part of the public Company API v4 surface", so the board must confirm with PeopleForce first. Test access is asked for through https://peopleforce.io/partners. Source: https://developer.peopleforce.io/careers/v1/guides/job-boards.
2. **Careers API.** Read-only list of a company's published vacancies at `app.peopleforce.io/api/careers/v1/vacancies`, needs a "Career API key" from the company. Source: https://developer.peopleforce.io/careers/v1/getting-started (search result summary).
3. **Join the VONQ catalogue** and be sold as a paid channel inside PeopleForce (and inside Greenhouse, Zoho Recruit, JazzHR and other systems that use VONQ). I found no public "add your job board" page at VONQ; its partner help pages returned 403. VONQ's FAQ for its free "Organic Posting Network" says partners can request a board "but there is no guarantee". Sources: search results for hapisupport.vonq.com; https://hapisupport.vonq.com/hc/en-us/articles/22294089087772-Integration (403, not opened).

**Reading their customers' jobs:** PeopleForce careers pages look like `{company}.peopleforce.io/careers` with jobs at `/careers/v/{id}-{slug}` (example: https://team.peopleforce.io/careers, 5 open jobs). Our collector reads only Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby (`collect.py`, `fetch_feed`). Most PeopleForce customers hire in Ukraine and Europe, so the gain for an English board is small. Whether these pages carry JobPosting data could not be checked (the reader shows text only).

## 4. What to take first

Most valuable first. All but item 4 can be built on the static site.

1. **Job description templates, one page per role, built from real postings.** PeopleForce has none (WebSearch `site:peopleforce.io job description template` finds only a glossary entry). We have live postings for 165 roles, so each page can show the duties and skills that real employers write, the pay range they state, and a "post this job free" button. It is the one page type that brings *employers* in from search, and it leads straight to our $29-59 posts.
2. **"How to get into {role}" pages for career changers.** This is the topic Careerist's ~450 blog posts and its whole business are built on (https://careerist.cc/insights; 71% of its students "had no tech background", https://careerist.cc/outcomes). We can answer it with numbers from today's postings: skills asked most, share of entry-level jobs, pay, open jobs now, resume example. Static, one template, many pages.
3. **A career quiz ("Which tech job fits me?").** Careerist puts "Take the Quiz" on its blog as a lead magnet. Ours would be plain JavaScript, no sign-up, and end on a role page with live jobs, the resume example and the Plus offer. Cheap, shareable, good for new visitors.
4. **Become a channel inside applicant tracking systems.** Two doors: ask VONQ how a board joins its paid catalogue (sold inside PeopleForce, Greenhouse, Zoho Recruit, JazzHR), and keep PeopleForce's documented job-board integration in mind (employer's API key → we read open jobs → we send applicants back tagged with our name; https://developer.peopleforce.io/careers/v1/guides/job-boards). First step is only an email by the owner; build only if terms fit. It puts our paid posts where recruiters already work.
5. **A bigger glossary, one page per term.** PeopleForce's main free asset is about 297 HR terms, each on its own page, in 4 languages (https://peopleforce.io/hr-glossary). Ours has 29 words on one page. Growing to 150+ job-seeker and hiring terms (notice period, at-will, background check, W-2 vs 1099, pay band, offer letter …), each with a two-sentence answer, suits Google and AI answers.
6. **"Keywords from a job description" as its own tool page.** Careerist has one page per keyword-sized tool (7 in `/free-tools/`). Our `resume-match` already does the work; a separate page with its own title and questions is a day of work for a common search.
7. **A referral program for communities and newsletters.** Careerist pays "up to $500" per student with a 90-day cookie (https://careerist.cc/ambassador-program). We could pay partners a share of the employer posts and Plus sales they bring, in crypto. Our `/partners/` page already invites these groups; the worker can track a referral code.

Not worth taking: courses, the job guarantee, recruiting at 15% of salary, webinars, sales calls, and all of PeopleForce's HR software.
