## 1. Careerist (careerist.cc)

**Same company as careerist.com?** Yes. `www.careerist.com` sends a 302 redirect to `https://careerist.cc/`. Source: WebFetch of https://www.careerist.com.

**In one line:** a paid online school that retrains US adults with no tech background for entry-level tech jobs (manual QA, QA automation, AI automation, tech sales). Every course ends with an internship, career coaching and job search help, backed by a money-back job guarantee. Everything free on the site exists to collect a phone number for a sales call.

### 1.2 How it is paid

- **One-time upfront** is pushed hardest: the biggest discount, and the only way to keep the job guarantee. The payments page says about 60% of clients pay upfront. Source: https://careerist.cc/payments.
- **In-house installments**: no interest, up to 4 months, at least 10% first, then 30/60/90 days, with automatic card billing. **Split in 2 or 3** payments within 28 days. Source: same.
- **Loans** through Climb Credit, Affirm and Klarna. The page says 70% of applicants are approved. Loan students lose the guarantee. Source: same.
- **Income share (ISA):** not offered on the payments page. The guarantee text still mentions "ISA students", so it existed before. Source: https://careerist.cc/legal/guarantee/05112026.
- **No subscription** anywhere.
- **Permanent "limited time" discounts.** Every course shows a crossed-out list price and an "Enroll by" date. The upfront price is the real price.

### 1.3 The job guarantee (full rules)

Source: https://careerist.cc/legal/guarantee/05112026 (latest of 11 dated versions since Nov 2021).

- Money back if no qualifying US job within **10 months** after the certificate. The home page still says "within a year".
- The student must send **80 applications a week** in months 1-6 and 40 a week in months 7-10, with screenshots as proof. Attend all 6 coaching calls. Grow LinkedIn to 500+ contacts. Score 90%+ on homework. Be willing to move anywhere in the US. Not change the resume without the coach's approval.
- Out: loan payers, people without US work rights, anyone who turns down a qualifying offer.
- Refund: 100% of tuition paid to Careerist. Fees for financing or card processing are not returned.

The guarantee is mostly a sales tool: the rules are strict enough that few people could claim it.

### 1.4 How they prove results

- **Ratings shown** (no review counts next to them): Facebook 4.8, SwitchUp 4.7, Trustpilot 4.7. The footer shows SwitchUp 4.6 and Google 4.5, so the two do not match. Course pages say "4.7 average rate from 2000 reviews". Sources: https://careerist.cc/success, https://careerist.cc/automation.
- **Outcome claims:** "1000+ graduates are employed", "89% of our grads found a Full-time job", "51% … work remotely", "42 states". No date range, no method, no audit (no CIRR). Source: https://careerist.cc/success. The tech sales page says "1,300+ Hired Graduates". Source: https://careerist.cc/tech-sales.
- **Salary claims are inconsistent:** QA Automation page shows "$115K" average and "$87K" average alumni salary on the same page. "Top salaries" $125k (QA), $167K (automation), $118K (sales engineering). Sources: https://careerist.cc/automation, https://careerist.cc/success.
- **Stories:** about 16 named graduates with "from → to" job titles (for example "Warehouse Associate" → "Software Tester"), some as videos, some as full blog stories. 15 to 42 employer logos, with a note that these firms do not endorse Careerist.
- **Media:** a "Media about us" page and a Forbes Business Council post in the blog (not opened).
