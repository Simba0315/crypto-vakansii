# What people dislike in job apps, and what it means for us (2026-11-13)

Asked by the owner: "look at the reviews of the apps of Indeed and the other big job companies, what people dislike, and build
what is missing". 364 negative reviews (1 to 3 stars) were read and sorted: Indeed 111, LinkedIn 56, Glassdoor 58, ZipRecruiter 44,
Dice 24, Snagajob 22, Handshake 22, Monster 10, Jooble 9, Upwork 8. One review can fall under several themes.

Star ratings hide the problems: they are inflated by in-app prompts (ZipRecruiter's histogram is 91% five-star, mostly one-word
reviews). Among the 100 newest written App Store reviews, 66 were negative for Indeed, 95 for LinkedIn, 56 for Glassdoor.

## Themes by number of mentions

1. Irrelevant results and recommendations: 66
2. Bugs, crashes, blank pages: 51
3. Scams, fake and ghost jobs, fake recruiters: 47
4. Missing filters and controls (salary, sort by date, job type, company search, hide a company, report a job): 47
5. No reply after applying: 40
6. Spam calls, texts and email after sign-up, data sold: 38
7. Forced account, mandatory phone number, login failures: 37
8. Notifications and emails that cannot be switched off: 36
9. Location and radius wrong: 35
10. Applying: redirects, re-typing, forms that fail, one-tap applies by accident: 31
11. AI forced on people (search, resume rewriting, interviews): 27
12. Saved jobs, searches or settings lost: 17
13. Duplicates and dismissed jobs coming back: 15
14. Stale or filled jobs: 12

What people praise: one profile and one tap to apply, a notice when an employer has seen the application (ZipRecruiter); the
number of listings (Indeed); all applications in one place and one-click save (Teal, Huntr); autofill of forms (Simplify).

## If we make an app or an installable site

Opens fast and offline to the last results and saved jobs; no sign-in or permission prompt on first open; notifications off
until switched on for one search; filters persist; saved data survives updates; shared links open the exact job; one clearly
labelled tap to the employer; no AI layer in front of search and no "rate us" pop-ups; updates never remove a filter.
